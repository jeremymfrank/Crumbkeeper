# Crumbkeeper v0.1.5 — Tester Preview

Crumbkeeper is a local-first Android cookbook for phones and tablets. No account required.

**Highlights**
- Cook/Share first; saved recipe editing is protected under More.
- Multiple independent Cooking Mode timers and foreground audible alerts.
- Copy/paste recipes from ChatGPT, and import local text, Crumbkeeper archives and PDFs.
- Bundled on-device PDF OCR (including scanned PDFs). Review all measurements and temperatures before saving.
- Whole-cookbook backup (`.crumbbook`) and individual recipe sharing (`.crumbrecipe`).
- Move recipes to Trash under More; restore from Settings → Trash. Permanent removal requires confirmation.

**Tester warning:** This is a debug APK, **not a production release**. Android locked-screen/background alarms are not yet supported. Do not rely on it for unattended cooking reminders. Back up your recipes outside your phone before future updates or app removal. Because debug signing is not yet stable, a future tester APK may need a separate install and backup/import to move recipes.

Use **Download Crumbkeeper-v0.1.5-testers.apk** under Assets. Share your Android version and any screenshots or sample PDFs when reporting issues.
